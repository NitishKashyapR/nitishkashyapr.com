New Joiner Onboarding Checklist

A standardized operational framework covering pre-boarding compliance, Day 1 orientation, week-1 assimilation, and 30-60-90 day performance checkpoints.
Formulated by Nitish Kashyap R · MBA (HR) Candidate · VIMTECH, Tumkur · nitishkashyapr8@gmail.com
Practitioner Context & Purpose: Formulated by Nitish Kashyap R (MBA Candidate, Specialization in Human Resources) as an operational reference framework. Onboarding is a decisive touchpoint in the employee journey: early confusion or administrative delay significantly elevates 90-day attrition risk. This toolkit bridges human warmth with rigorous process coordination—ensuring complete statutory documentation, punctual IT enablement, peer buddy alignment, and structured performance touchpoints.

1 Phase 1: Pre-Boarding Operations (Offer Acceptance to T-1 Day)

The pre-boarding period sets the professional tone of the organization and mitigates offer drop-out risk. The coordinator oversees document verification, IT provisioning, and proactive candidate engagement:

✓
1. Welcome Call & Welcome Kit Briefing (Within 48h of Offer Acceptance)
Initiate a friendly check-in call with candidate; congratulate them; outline pre-boarding milestones, expected joining date, office reporting address, and point-of-contact details.
Owner: HR Coordinator | SLA: T+2 Days
✓
2. Statutory Compliance Documentation Collection & Audit
Collect soft copies and verify authenticity of mandatory Indian statutory records: PAN Card, Aadhaar Card, UAN/EPFO Number, Bank Account details, Degree Certificates, and previous employer Relieving Letter.
Owner: HR Operations | SLA: T-7 Days
✓
3. Third-Party Background Verification (BGV) Hand-off
Initiate BGV case file with authorized verification partner covering address verification, criminal record check, and past employment history.
Owner: BGV Partner / HR Operations | SLA: T-7 Days
✓
4. IT Asset, Email, & Software Provisioning Ticket
Submit formal IT Helpdesk request for laptop provisioning, standard software suite installation, official corporate email ID creation, and Slack/Teams channel permissions. For remote joiners, track courier shipment tracking number.
Owner: IT Systems Support | SLA: T-3 Days
✓
5. Onboarding Buddy Selection & Pre-Joining Introduction
Coordinate with hiring manager to assign an onboarding peer buddy; send pre-joining intro email connecting buddy and new joiner to answer informal cultural/commute questions.
Owner: Department Manager / HR | SLA: T-2 Days
Pre-Boarding Governance Standard: Ensures full compliance with statutory documentation lead-times under the Employees' Provident Funds (EPF) Act and Payment of Gratuity Act. Weekly candidate check-ins during notice periods reduce candidate no-show probability by up to 35%.

2 Phase 2: Day 1 Structured Orientation Schedule

A structured, welcoming itinerary designed to eliminate first-day anxiety, ensure statutory form sign-offs, and facilitate meaningful team immersion:

Time Slot Session Title Operational Details & Objectives Facilitator
09:30 AM – 10:15 AM Front Desk Welcome & Access Reception greeting, photo capture for biometric ID card, physical access badge encoding, desk allocation, and welcome swag kit handover. Facilities / HR
10:15 AM – 11:30 AM Document Audit & Form Signing In-person verification of original certificates; physical signing of Gratuity nomination (Form F), EPFO Declaration (Form 11), NDA, and Code of Conduct. HR Operations
11:30 AM – 01:00 PM IT Hardware Handover & SSO Laptop handover, asset receipt sign-off, enterprise SSO login setup, multi-factor authentication (MFA) enrollment, password policy, and VPN testing. IT Support
01:00 PM – 02:15 PM Team Welcome Lunch Informal lunch hosted by assigned Onboarding Buddy and immediate team members to foster peer belonging and psychological comfort. Buddy & Team
02:15 PM – 03:45 PM HR Policy & Culture Briefing Interactive overview of office timings, hybrid work guidelines, attendance logging (HRMS), leave policy (Earned, Sick, Casual), and POSH awareness. HR Specialist
04:00 PM – 05:00 PM Hiring Manager Alignment First formal 1-on-1 check-in with department manager; walk-through of team mission, immediate Week 1 assignments, and introductory schedule. Reporting Manager
05:00 PM – 05:30 PM Day 1 Wrap-up & Check-out Quick coordinator check-in: resolve any pending laptop or access glitches; confirm Day 2 start time and arrival details. HR Operations

3 Phase 3: Week 1 Integration Milestones

Focuses on role clarity, tool competence, and cultural immersion through structured touchpoints:

✓
Daily 15-Minute Buddy Syncs
Informal morning touchpoint between new hire and buddy to address navigation, lunch routines, organizational acronyms, and practical day-to-day workflow questions.
Cadence: Daily (Mon–Fri) | Facilitator: Onboarding Peer Buddy
✓
Stakeholder Introductory 1-on-1s
Brief 20-minute introductory meetings with cross-functional partners (Finance, Operations, Marketing, Product) to build organizational context and cross-team relationships.
Cadence: Days 2 to 4 | Facilitator: Hiring Manager / Joiner
✓
Mandatory Compliance & InfoSec Learning Modules
Self-paced completion of Prevention of Sexual Harassment (POSH) training, Data Privacy (GDPR/DPDP Act) modules, and Information Security protocols on company LMS.
Deadline: Day 5 (5:00 PM) | Facilitator: L&D / Compliance
✓
Week 1 Pulse Check Survey (Friday 3:00 PM)
A brief 4-question pulse survey sent via HRMS: (1) Were your tools ready? (2) Did you feel welcomed by team? (3) Understand immediate role goals? (4) What can HR support next week?
SLA: Friday EOD | Facilitator: HR Operations Coordinator

4 Phase 4: 30-60-90 Day Milestone Roadmap & Probation Review

A progressive roadmap providing role clarity, incremental autonomy, and an objective basis for probation confirmation:

Day 30 Milestone
Absorption & Learning
Focus: Understanding internal processes, shadowing peer tasks, and executing basic workflows under supervision.
  • 100% compliance modules completed.
  • Understands primary SOPs and file structures.
  • Participates actively in team stand-ups.
HR Action: 30-Day Check-in with Joiner & Manager
Day 60 Milestone
Autonomous Execution
Focus: Managing daily deliverables independently; solving routine challenges; suggesting operational improvements.
  • Independent execution with < 5% oversight.
  • Constructive collaboration with cross-teams.
  • Identifies any tool or knowledge gaps.
HR Action: Mid-Probation Performance Feedback Sync
Day 90 Milestone
Full Ownership & Confirmation
Focus: Consistent goal delivery; full ownership of assigned metrics; ready for formal confirmation of employment.
  • Meets role KPIs established at intake.
  • Peer & manager feedback collected.
  • Formal probation assessment completed.
HR Action: Issue Formal Probation Confirmation Letter

5 Operational Troubleshooting Guide for HR Coordinators

Practical protocols for handling common onboarding contingencies encountered in corporate HR operations:

Contingency Scenario Root Cause & Operational Risk Coordinator Resolution Protocol
Laptop Hardware Delay (Remote Joiner) Courier transit delay causes new hire to sit idle without access on Day 1, creating negative first impression. Notify joiner 24h prior; provide temp access via web client / mobile authenticator; coordinate with IT for cloud-based virtual desktop environment until courier delivery.
EPFO / UAN Discrepancy (Previous Employer) Name or Date of Birth mismatch in Aadhaar vs. EPFO database prevents automatic PF account transfer. Obtain previous member ID; issue Joint Declaration Form to regional PF commissioner; assist employee in online Aadhaar-UAN seeding via EPFO portal.
Joiner Feeling Isolated / Disengaged Remote team busy in sprint deadlines; joiner does not receive sufficient attention during week 1. Intervene proactively after Week 1 survey; schedule immediate check-in with department lead; re-assign a more proactive peer buddy.
Missing Relieving Letter from Prior Firm Previous organization delayed exit clearance documentation, holding up formal employee file completion. Collect formal resignation acceptance email and final salary slips as provisional proof; grant a 30-day provisional grace period with employee undertaking.
Process Assurance Note: Designed in compliance with standard Indian employment statutory requirements (PF/EPFO, Gratuity Form F, POSH) and best-in-class employee experience standards.
