2 Structured Job Description (JD)

Role Objective

The HR Operations Coordinator serves as the administrative backbone of our hiring lifecycle. The incumbent manages inbound candidate pipelines, coordinates multi-stage panel interviews across Google Calendar and Microsoft Teams, verifies early applicant documentation, and maintains meticulous tracker hygiene to ensure a respectful, high-touch candidate experience.

Key Duties & Operational Deliverables

  • Pipeline Screening: Review inbound resumes on ATS against defined educational and foundational skill criteria within a 48-hour SLA.
  • Interview Logistics: Coordinate interview schedules between hiring managers and prospective candidates; resolve scheduling conflicts proactively.
  • Candidate Communication: Issue timely status updates, interview briefing packs, and feedback summaries to eliminate candidate ghosting.
  • Tracker Hygiene: Maintain daily updates across applicant spreadsheets (source tag, screening status, scorecard completion, offer release).
  • Pre-Offer Verification: Collect and catalog primary verification credentials (marks cards, ID proofs, relieving letters, internship certificates).

Success Metrics & SLAs

Target initial resume screening turnaround: ≤ 48 hours; Candidate interview attendance rate: ≥ 90%; Data entry error rate across trackers: 0%.

3 Dual-Tier Screening Criteria Matrix

To prevent recruiter bias and reduce downstream panel fatigue, candidate profiles are evaluated against strict objective parameters:

Must-Have Criteria (Non-Negotiable)
  • Academic Foundation: Regular full-time Bachelor's degree (B.Com, BBA, BBM, or equivalent) with consistent academic performance.
  • Spreadsheet Proficiency: Proven command of Google Sheets or MS Excel (VLOOKUP, Pivot Tables, conditional formatting, data validation).
  • Written Articulation: Clear, grammatically sound professional English correspondence without jargon or typographical errors.
  • Information Discretion: Strict commitment to maintaining applicant confidentiality and data privacy (handling compensation and contact data).
  • Location Feasibility: Resident of Bangalore/Tumkur region or immediate readiness for hybrid office attendance.
Good-to-Have Criteria (Value-Add)
  • ATS Exposure: Hands-on familiarity with modern ATS workflows (Greenhouse, Lever, Zoho Recruit, or Workday).
  • Verified Certifications: Completed foundational coursework in HR management, talent sourcing, or people analytics (SHRM, HRCI, Google).
  • Campus Coordination: Prior experience participating in college placement committees, event logistics, or team leadership.
  • AI-Assisted Productivity: Experience utilizing structured prompts for job description drafting and email correspondence refinement.

4 Standardized Candidate Evaluation Scorecard

Evaluation scoring is anchored on a 1-to-5 scale (1 = Unsatisfactory, 3 = Meets Expectations, 5 = Exceptional) with explicit behavioral benchmarks to calibrate panel feedback:

Process Discipline & Attention to Detail 25% Demonstrates systematic task tracking using checklists; accurately logs dates, names, and status tags in demo spreadsheet without prompts. Anticipates workflow bottlenecks; proactively identifies tracker discrepancy and establishes self-auditing routines to maintain data integrity.
Written & Verbal Communication 25% Communicates with professional tone, clarity, and grammatical precision; handles phone queries courteously and accurately summarizes call notes. Demonstrates exceptional empathy and executive presence; handles candidate frustration or ambiguity with diplomacy and poise.
Ethics & Data Discretion 20% Articulates clear understanding of candidate confidential information; treats salary details, phone numbers, and resumes with strict privacy. Demonstrates deep grasp of compliance and POSH/statutory norms; can navigate ethical dilemmas involving peer pressure or unauthorized data requests.
Coordination & Follow-Through 15% Follows up on pending tasks methodically; sends calendar invites and reminder nudges within established operational SLAs. Multitasks seamlessly across 15+ concurrent applicant workflows; manages sudden interviewer cancellations with zero candidate friction.
Learning Agility & Tool Readiness 15% Comfortable learning new HR software; open to feedback; possesses baseline knowledge of modern spreadsheets and digital workplace tools. Independent learner who self-upskills via certified programs; quickly leverages modern digital tools to improve administrative speed.

Qualifying Threshold: Composite score ≥ 3.4 / 5.0 with no single competency rated below 2.5 is required to advance to manager round.

5 15-Minute Telephone Screening Protocol

Conducted by the HR Coordinator to validate logistical availability, communication clarity, and candidate interest prior to panel scheduling:

1. Opening & Context Setting 2 Mins · Objective: Rapport
"Hello [Candidate Name], this is Nitish from People Operations at [Company Name]. Calling regarding your application for the HR Operations Coordinator role. Is this a convenient 10-15 minute window to speak?"
2. Role Motivation & Career Orientation 4 Mins · Objective: Alignment
"We noted your academic background in commerce/management. What specifically drew your interest to HR operations, and how does this role align with where you want to build your early career foundation?"
3. Spreadsheet Proficiency & Process Hygiene 4 Mins · Objective: Technical Competence
"Managing interview logs and candidate records in Google Sheets is a daily routine here. Could you share an example of a project where you used spreadsheets to track data, coordinate schedules, or calculate metrics?"
4. Situational Agility & Escalation Discipline 3 Mins · Objective: Problem Solving
"Suppose an interviewer has not joined a scheduled virtual meeting 5 minutes past start time, and the candidate is waiting in the room. What exact steps would you take?" (Look for proactive panelist contact, candidate reassurance, and manager escalation).
5. Logistics & Availability Confirmation 2 Mins · Objective: Feasibility
Confirm residential location, commute feasibility to Bengaluru office, notice period (≤ 15 days), and alignment with ₹4.20–₹5.40 LPA budget band.
Screening Gatekeeper Standard: Candidates advancing to panel interviews must demonstrate fluent English articulation, confirmed ≤ 15-day joining availability, and verified baseline spreadsheet comfort.

7 Recruiter Day-of-Interview Execution Checklist

A rigorous operational checklist for the talent acquisition coordinator to eliminate scheduling glitches and ensure 100% SLA compliance:

  • ✓
    T-24 Hours (Day Prior): Send calendar reconfirmation and dial-in links to candidate and panel; verify resume is attached to the calendar invite.
  • ✓
    T-1 Hour: Verify that the primary panelist has accepted the calendar invite; identify back-up panelist if primary is in conflicting client meetings.
  • ✓
    T-10 Minutes: Log in to virtual meeting room; confirm audio/video clarity and ensure waiting room admittance settings are active.
  • ✓
    T+0 Minutes: Admit candidate punctually; deliver warm 1-minute welcome and introduce panel before handing over meeting floor.
  • ✓
    T+2 Hours (Post-Meeting): Collect structured scorecard ratings and written notes from panel; record composite score in recruitment tracker; trigger candidate status update within 24 hours.
Candidate Experience Commitment: Enforces a strict 48-hour response turnaround SLA across all application stages. Every candidate receives transparent, respectful communication to protect employer branding and eliminate ghosting.
